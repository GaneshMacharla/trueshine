⚙️ Precision Protocol

Our 6-Step Mechanised Cleaning Process

Every tank goes through an exhaustive multi-stage protocol to guarantee absolute purity.

01 Submersible Pump

Mechanised Dewatering

High-flow submersible drainage pumps rapidly empty stagnant water from your tank or sump without wasting time.

02 Slurry Pump

Sludge & Silt Extraction

Heavy-duty industrial slurry pumps extract settled mud, sand, dead organic matter, and thick floor sediment.

03 150+ Bar Pressure

Rotary High-Pressure Jet Wash

150+ bar rotary water jets scour the ceiling, walls, corners, and floor, stripping away green algae and biofilm.

04 Industrial Vac

Vacuum Cleaning & Muck Suction

Industrial wet suction removes every last drop of dirty slurry and detached grime, leaving the tank pristine.

05 Food-Grade Safe

Food-Grade Antibacterial Spray

Interior is treated with non-toxic, government-approved antibacterial sanitizers that eliminate fungal spores.

06 Medical UV

UV Radiator Sterilization

Medical-grade germicidal ultraviolet radiation penetrates all crevices to kill 99.9% of bacteria (E. coli) and viruses.
