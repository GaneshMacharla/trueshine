⚙️ Precision Engineering

Our 6-Step Mechanised Cleaning & Sanitization Process

Every tank goes through an exhaustive multi-stage protocol to ensure absolute purity and hygiene.

01 Step 1

Mechanised Dewatering

High-flow submersible drainage pumps rapidly empty the dirty, stagnant water from your overhead tank or sump without wasting valuable time.

02 Step 2

Sludge & Silt Extraction

Industrial heavy-duty slurry pumps extract settled mud, rust, dead organic particles, and thick sediment deposits directly from the bottom floor.

03 Step 3

High-Pressure Rotary Jet Wash

150+ bar rotary high-pressure water jets scour the ceiling, walls, corners, and floor, stripping away stubborn green algae and biofilm layers completely.

04 Step 4

Vacuum Cleaning & Muck Suction

Wet industrial vacuum suction removes every last drop of dirty slurry, detached grime, and particulate matter, leaving the tank pristine and dry.

05 Step 5

Food-Grade Antibacterial Spray

Tank interior is sprayed with specialized, non-toxic, government-approved antibacterial sanitizers that eliminate fungal spores and microscopic pathogens.

06 Final UV Polish

UV Radiator Sterilization

Medical-grade ultraviolet (UV) germicidal radiator treatment penetrates every crevice to eradicate 99.9% of bacteria (E. coli, coliform) and viruses.

⚠️ Did You Know?

An Uncleaned Water Tank is a Breeding Ground for Disease

According to health researchers, municipal water carries tiny sediment, dissolved silt, and pipe rust into your tank daily. Over 6 months, this creates thick black bottom sludge and green wall biofilm.

🦠

Bacterial Biofilms (E. Coli & Coliform)

Can cause stomach infections, diarrhea, dysentery, and food poisoning.

🚿

Skin Irritations & Hair Fall

Bathing in contaminated, stagnant tank water leads to itchy skin rashes and accelerated hair breakage.

🧱

Pipe Clogging & Foul Odor

Sediment blocks concealed pipes, geysers, and shower nozzles, leading to expensive plumbing repairs.
